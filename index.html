progress, documented

the timeline

From silicon breakthroughs to climate commitments; scroll through the moments that moved the mission forward.

scroll to explore · hover or focus on a card for details

01 / origin
1968

1968

Intel founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

02 / breakthrough
4004

1971

first microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting the microprocessor revolution and propelling the future of computing devices.

03 / architecture
x86

1978

8086 processor

The 8086 establishes the x86 architecture that continues to drive countless PCs and servers in the modern era.

04 / performance
32-bit

1985

386 processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, ushering in a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

05 / turning point
peak

2006

peak GHG emissions

This year marks Intel's highest annual operational greenhouse gas emissions. Investments in abatement, renewable energy, and efficient manufacturing begin reversing the trend.

06 / strategy
RISE

2020

RISE strategy

Intel launches its Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling strategy and 2030 goals to drive climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

07 / commitment
net 0

2022

net-zero by 2040

Intel commits to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across Scope 1 and 2 global operations by 2040, building on years of environmental initiatives.

08 / energy
99%

2023

renewable electricity

The company achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, lowering carbon emissions and driving progress toward long-term sustainability goals.

09 / collective action
future

2024

sustainability summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, uniting suppliers, government officials, and industry leaders around sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.
